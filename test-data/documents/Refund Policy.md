# Refund Policy

## 1. General Principles
This policy sets forth the conditions, timelines, and authorization levels under which customer refunds, billing adjustments, and credits are approved and processed.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- **Subscription Services**: Full refund available within 30 days of initial purchase if service uptime falls below 99.5% or service fails to deliver agreed capabilities.
- **Duplicate Charges**: 100% refund credited immediately upon verification of duplicate transaction IDs.
- **Service Request Billing Disputes**: If an erroneous charge is confirmed by billing audit, full refund or billing credit will be issued within 3-5 business days.
- **Non-Refundable Items**: One-time implementation consulting fees and customized engineering development costs are strictly non-refundable once delivery milestone sign-off is completed.

## 3. Approval Matrix & Limits
- **Frontline Support Specialist**: Authorized to issue billing credits up to \$100.00 USD.
- **Support Team Lead / Manager**: Authorized to issue refunds up to \$1,000.00 USD.
- **Finance Director / VP**: Required for all refund requests exceeding \$1,000.00 USD.

## 4. Processing Timelines & Method
- Approved refunds are credited back to the original payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer) within 5 to 7 business days.
- Processing fees are waived on all verified billing error corrections.
