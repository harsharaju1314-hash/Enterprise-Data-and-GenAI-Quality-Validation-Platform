# Customer Support Policy

## 1. Scope & Objective
This policy defines the standard operating procedures, communication channels, and support tiers for assisting enterprise customers across all product lines.

## 2. Operating Hours & Channels
- **Standard Support**: Available Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM EST via Email and Web Portal.
- **Priority Support**: Available 24/7/365 for Platinum Tier customers via dedicated phone line and direct Slack/Teams bridge.
- **Self-Service Portal**: Available 24/7 for ticket creation, status checking, and knowledge base search.

## 3. Customer Tier Entitlements
- **Platinum Tier**: Dedicated Customer Success Manager (CSM), 1-hour initial response SLA for urgent tickets, quarterly business reviews, and 24/7 phone access.
- **Gold Tier**: 2-hour initial response SLA for urgent tickets, email and web portal access, monthly service reports.
- **Silver Tier**: 4-hour initial response SLA for urgent tickets, business-hours email and web portal access.
- **Bronze Tier**: 8-hour initial response SLA, community and web portal ticket access.

## 4. Ticket Lifecycle
All incoming inquiries are assigned a unique Request ID (format: `SR-XXXX`). The lifecycle states are:
1. **Open**: Ticket received and queued for triage.
2. **In_Progress**: Engineer assigned and actively investigating.
3. **Pending_Customer**: Awaiting additional information or verification from customer.
4. **Resolved**: Solution implemented and provided to customer.
5. **Closed**: Customer confirmed resolution or 5 business days passed without customer reply after resolution.

## 5. Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)
Upon ticket closure, customers receive an automated survey scoring from 1 (Very Dissatisfied) to 5 (Very Satisfied). CSAT scores below 3 automatically trigger a supervisor review within 24 hours.
